With defining a reversal, I focus on price taking out its previous candles high or low
and then closing back into the range. This allows me to anticipate the following candle
to have a continuation. So if price has a bearish reversal here, there is a bearish
continuation following or a bullish reversal here, a bullish continuation following that.
Now with a continuation, I am anticipating price not to reverse as it took out its previous
candles low and closed outside. So if I'm anticipating price to continue, then we would just continue
lower. So you can see here we close over our previous candles high and then we continue in the following candle.
