To understand the fractal model you have to understand what swing points are and how to
number them. So let's go over that. When we have a swing low, candle two is always going to be the
low. Candle three will always follow candle two, candle four will follow candle three, and candle one
will always be the candle prior to the low or candle two. Now in a bearish swing point,
candle two will always be the high candle three will follow as well as candle four,
and candle one will always be the candle prior to candle two. Now that you understand the foundation
of my model, you can better understand what the numbers mean when labeling them.
