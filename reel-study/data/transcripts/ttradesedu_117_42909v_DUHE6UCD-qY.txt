If I'm looking to trade a reversal at the 930 open, there is a particular sequence I want to see.
I want to see the higher timeframe candle have a small wick and then 930 create a change in
the state of delivery on the lower timeframe. This can be formed as a closure right at 930 open
or it can make an opposing run and then close over. Following this change in the state of
delivery on the lower timeframe, I'm then going to be looking for 930 to tend to expand
higher to fill out this expansion candle on the hourly or 30 minute chart.
