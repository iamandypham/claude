than a bad trade ever could.

Traders with solid technical analysis skills
losing money because they let their emotions take over.

leaving thousands of dollars on the table.
Greed pushes you to over trade
taking setups that don't make you much money.
But the worst emotion by far is hopes.
Start praying and that kind of thing.
Hope that losing trades will come back
instead of cutting your losses
when your system tells you to.

My biggest losses weren't from a bad analysis.
They were from letting my emotions override my system.
This is exactly why having a mechanical approach
is so critical.
It removes emotion from the equation entirely.

that you take your first step
to being a profitable trader.
