So what is the difference between high resistance and low resistance liquidity?
And why is it important with high resistance liquidity?
We have a swing high or low that has liquidity resting.
It is then swept out as price reverses.
And now we have liquidity resting on that new high or low.
Now with low resistance liquidity, we have that same swing high or swing low
with the liquidity resting, but price fails to reach up and sweep out that liquidity.
So now we have a build up of liquidity.
So why is this important?
I want to use low resistance liquidity or these failure swings as a target.
And I want to use high resistance liquidity or these sweeps as a spot for my stop loss.
