An order block is a single or series of up close candles into an important level before price
displaces and closes below the opening price of that single or series of up close candles,
validating it as an order block. Then when retested, I'm looking for price to trade away
from that order block. Similarly, with a bullish order block, we have a series or
single down close candle into an important level, which then gets displaced and closed
over validating it as an order block. Then when price retests this area, I want to see it
trade away. A propulsion block is an order block off of another order block. So for this example here,
we have a down close candle into an important level, which is then closed over validating it as an
order block, then price retraces and reaches into this order block, then closing over this down
close or series of down close candles into this order block that then creates a propulsion block.
Now with a propulsion block, I anticipate the opening price to be very sensitive. I do not
want to see price break the mean threshold of a propulsion block.
