The first time I had ever cried from trading was when I first made the transition to live
and everything felt like it was falling apart. It felt like everything I'd learned was just
down the drain. None of it made sense. I was taking losses and had to go back to SIM.
It was just such a disappointing, devastating realization. Such a bummer. I was living
in San Diego working at the YMCA and working for a audiovisual company as like an admin assistant.
I think I also started my own jewelry business simultaneously and I was making
abalone shell necklaces and selling them. And the brand was called Victoria Lee Abalone.
I believe there's still an Instagram account for that jewelry. But that was the first
time trading made me cry was when I first went live and I felt like everything I had learned,
everything I was practicing just didn't, didn't click. Wasn't working. I knew nothing. I felt stupid.
I didn't see the light at the end of the tunnel. I was like, this is, this is an ultimate bummer.
Your psychology whenever you go into live trading is so much different. So much different.
You are a fragile little baby when you make the transition from SIM to live.
