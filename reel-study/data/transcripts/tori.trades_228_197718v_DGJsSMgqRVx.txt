I wish someone had told me in my 20s,
take the biggest risks of your entire life right now.
Remember, you have time.
And in your 20s, you can get so scared
and you can be so easily lulled into the idea
that good is good enough,
that feeling safe and comfortable
is what you should always be looking for.
That way of thinking in your 20s in particular,
dead fucking wrong.
I want you to start to create this mindset
of taking risks and going big.
