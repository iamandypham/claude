What is your trading strategy?

and ride that out for its entirety
and then rinse and repeat, rinse and repeat,
rinse and repeat.
What's cool about my strategy specifically
is that it doesn't predict.
I'm not trying to guess when the markets
are gonna continue to trend
or when the markets are going to reverse
or pull back or whatever it may be.
I am allowing price to signal me or show me
or give me an indication of when there might be
a potential trend reversal
or if we're just continuing along the trend
that we're already in.
I'm trying to identify trends.
I'm looking for touch points,
potential areas of resistance with the trend lines,
areas where the price is likely to get to this trend line
and respect it or it gets to this trend line
and then violates it.
And then if it violates it,
this is a potential trend reversal
that I may be anticipating
