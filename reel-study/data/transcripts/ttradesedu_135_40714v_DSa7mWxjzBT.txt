Top-down analysis is when I combine multiple timeframes in the same direction to increase
my probability of a trade working out.
So you can see here, I first want to identify a bias.
That is where I choose a direction on the day.
But on my daily chart, is it bullish?
Is it bearish?
Is it likely to expand lower or expand higher?
Once I have identified that, in this case, I'm anticipating price to expand lower.
I then want to go down to my hourly chart to look for structure that aligns with
that.
I want to wait for the hourly chart to become bearish.
If it is still bullish, I wait for it to be bearish.
Once I have a change in the state of delivery and a point of interest, then I can go look
for an entry on my five minute timeframe.
And what you'll notice is all of these timeframes are pointing down or bearish.
This is then when expansion occurs.
