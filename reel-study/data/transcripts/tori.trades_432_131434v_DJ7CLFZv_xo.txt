Once you close the trade, remove yourself from the charts.
Go do a workout, go on a walk, go read a book,
go on a hike, go play with your dog,
meet up with some friends, remove yourself
and distract yourself for a moment.
If I continue to look at the charts
and I watch it move back in my favor,
it will start to create some very bad habits
of me regretting closing my trade,
wishing I had held it,
and then that resulting in me
making improper decisions in trades moving forward,
holding longer, thinking the last time
if I had held a little bit longer,
it moved in my favor.
Having those emotions and those habits
translated into the next trades will kill your account.
It will absolutely blow your account,
drain your account if you utilize
those same thinking patterns
or those habits in your future trades.
