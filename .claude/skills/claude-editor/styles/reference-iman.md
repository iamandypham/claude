# Style: reference-iman (Draft, 10 Nov 2026; not passed by Andy yet)

Learned from 15 public Iman Gadzhi clips with the reference-study skill: 8 TikToks from Oct-Nov 2026 (18-42 s) and 4 YouTube videos (first 6 min + 3 min mid samples of wtDDjBOfTZo, vOBKA8n15vg, WO-Gl6-6gLE; all of YZ6OyZKrRh8). Data: /mnt/project-files/content/rea-video-iman-2026-11-10/data/iman-agg.json. Small sample, one month of his output.
Style only. None of his footage, music, voice, logos or brand colours. Key: [M] measured by tool, [S] seen in frames, [?] not checked.

## A. Short-form (9:16, 18-42 s)
Pacing
- [M] 24-43 cuts per minute, median 30. Median shot 1.4 s (range 1.2-2.6). No shot over 4 s in 6 of 8 clips.
- [S] 5 of 8 are two-person podcast clips: cut on every change of speaker, each person in their own tight close-up. 2 are solo talking head, 1 is a two-person debate with b-roll.
- [M] No silence over 0.35 s in any of the 8. Pauses are cut out.
Captions
- [M] 2 words per caption (max 3-4), on screen about 0.5 s, 66-107 captions per minute.
- [M] Vertical centre at 51-58% of frame height (median 52%), so chest height, not the bottom third. Text height 3.0-4.8% of frame height (about 58-92 px at 1920). Small.
- [S] Sentence case with punctuation kept. Clean bold grotesque sans, no box, no outline, soft drop shadow. Hard cut in and out, no bounce or word-by-word highlight.
- [S] Two speakers: one white, one yellow, fixed for the whole clip. Solo clips: white only.
- [S] Solo clips: one key word or phrase every 4-8 s swaps to red italic serif and is 2-3x bigger than the rest ("smartest.", "destination.", "years.").
Titles
- [S] 3 of 8 open with a small one-line title at the very top (y about 10%) for the first 4-5 s, then it leaves. The other 5 have no title.
B-roll and graphics
- [S] Debate clip: b-roll or a simple graphic on about 1 shot in 3, each 1-2.5 s, always showing the exact noun being said (watch, chart, box).
- [S] Solo clip: plain white explainer cards (thin black line art, one label) for 2-4 s between dark talking-head shots.
- [S] Podcast clips: almost no b-roll (one 2 s insert in 5 clips).
Zooms and colour
- [S] Framing changes on cuts (wide to tight on the same speaker), not as animated zooms. Slow push-ins exist but are subtle.
- [S] Black-and-white for 1.5-3 s on a reaction or punchline in 4 of 8, with captions unchanged. One solo clip is black-and-white throughout with red as the only colour.
- [S] One accent colour per clip.
Sound
- [M] Music detected under more than a quarter of the runtime in 4 of 8; none or almost none in the other 4. Where there is music, cuts land on the beat no more often than chance. Loudness -14 to -21 LUFS integrated.
- [?] Sound effects: the tool lists pops and whooshes on caption changes, but it says itself those are mostly false hits. Not confirmed by ear.

## B. Long-form (16:9 YouTube)
- [M] First 6 minutes: 14-25 cuts per minute, median shot 1.8-3.5 s. Mid-video: 9-21 cuts per minute, median shot 2.1-5.4 s. The intro is cut about twice as fast as the body.
- [S] Intro pattern in the first 60 s of both tutorial videos: talking head 3-7 s, then animation or motion graphic 3-5 s, repeating. He is covered by b-roll or graphics on about half the shots in the first minute, about a quarter mid-video.
- [S] No running captions on talking head. Instead a short key phrase types on in thin italic serif, top-left or centre, 1-2 lines, a few times a minute.
- [S] Proof moments: 4-10 news-headline screenshots at 0.3-0.5 s each with the number highlighted, then one held for 3 s.
- [S] Screen recordings fill the frame with him as a circle or rounded tile in a bottom corner. Screens run 10-28 s, far longer than any other shot.
- [S] Recurring progress graphic (level card, percent complete) every few minutes.
- [S] One accent colour per video carried through lighting, graphics and highlights.
- [M] Music under 7-37% of the runtime; cuts not on the beat. Tutorial loudness -9 to -14 LUFS, documentary -15 to -17.5.
- [S] Documentary style (WO-Gl6-6gLE, YZ6OyZKrRh8): 5-word subtitles at the bottom (y 88%) for scene audio, b&w inserts, photo-frame borders. Not our use case.

## C. Pass/fail for a BG short cut in this style
- [ ] Median shot 1.2-2.6 s; no shot over 4 s unless it is a screen share
- [ ] No pause over 0.35 s; cut points still at audio dips (R-103)
- [ ] Captions: 2 words (max 4), sentence case, punctuation kept, breaks at sentence ends and cuts (R-102)
- [ ] Captions centred at 50-58% of frame height, text height 3-5% of frame, no box, never over the mouth or eyes
- [ ] One colour per speaker; solo = white
- [ ] Solo: one emphasis word every 4-8 s in the accent colour, italic serif, bigger. Real words from the script only
- [ ] B-roll shows the noun being said and lasts 1-2.5 s; our own footage, screenshots or licensed stock only
- [ ] One accent colour for the whole cut
- [ ] Hook title still from styles/hook-titles.md (Andy 10 Nov: "only use these styles")
- [ ] -14 LUFS (R-105); music only from the license ledger; 0.3 s end hold (R-104)
- [ ] SKILL.md section 4 self-check and ad rules pass

## D. Conflicts with our rules (ours win until Andy rules)
1. Hook title: his is small, at the top, gone after 4-5 s, or absent. Ours is a Brian Mark title that stays for a single-shot take.
2. Punch-in on cuts: on 1080p footage that is an upscale, which SKILL.md bans. Needs 4K source, or shoot wider and crop.
3. Caption size: his are about half the height of our 78 px ExtraBold caps. Smaller text may read worse on a paid ad with sound off. Test before adopting on ads.
4. Speaker cuts need two cameras or two people; most of our footage is one person, one camera.
5. Music: nothing licensed in the ledger yet.

## E. Test on our footage (10 Nov, Louis IMG_0912, draft louis-views-t1-ref-iman-v2)
- Captions pass: 39 captions, 2 words each, sentence case, 4 emphasis words in red italic serif.
- Caption position: his 52% centre landed on Louis's forehead, because Louis films himself with his face in the lower half of the frame. Moved to 38% (between the hook title and his hair). Rule: put captions in the clear band nearest the 50-58% line that misses eyes and mouth; check the frame sheet every time.
- Pacing fails his range: one camera, 6 shots, median 3.55 s, longest 7.3 s (his: median 1.4 s, nothing over 4 s). Reaching it needs b-roll, a second angle or 4K punch-ins. None exist for this clip.

## F. How to render it
`scripts/build_cut.py` with `"cap_style": "ref"` in the config: Inter SemiBold 60 px, sentence case, groups of 2, centre at `cap_center_pct` (default 54; set it per clip from the frame sheet), optional `emph` list of words set in red italic serif. Example: examples/louis-views-t1_ref.json.
