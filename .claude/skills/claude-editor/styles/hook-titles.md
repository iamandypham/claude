# Hook titles: the only styles we use (adopted 10 Nov 2026)

Andy, 10 Nov 2026 9:37 AM ET: "Steal his hook title styles and build it into our content system and only use these styles vc ours are ass" (link: instagram.com/therealbrianmark).

Source: 23 of Brian Mark's reels (@therealbrianmark), pulled 10 Nov 2026 (Virlo list + public embed video, frames at 0.3 s, 1.5 s, 3 s and one late frame). Frames: source-frames/grid0-5.jpg and late.jpg in this folder. We rewrite his patterns for trading Discord owners; we never reuse his exact lines.

Everything here applies to every BG video: organic reels, YouTube Shorts, ad videos, VSL cutdowns. Old hook title styles are superseded (list at the end).

## 1. The rules every title follows

1. Every talking-head video gets a hook title. (Supersedes "a hook title goes up only when it is a strong headline; otherwise none".)
2. One idea, 3-9 words, 1-2 lines. Two templates may run longer (T8 confession, T10 quote setup): up to about 20 words, 3 lines.
3. At most one emoji, at the end of the line: 🤯 🔥 ❌ ☠️ ‼️ ✅. None on ads if the ad account rejects emojis.
4. Title Case for listicles (T4). Sentence case for the rest. ONE word may go ALL CAPS for stress ("DEAD", "WORST").
5. Optional sub-line in brackets under the title, smaller: "(Full Blueprint)", "(Step by Step)".
6. Real figures only. A number in the title must be a sourced BG number (messaging bank, proof library) and spoken in the video. "$20M+" is allowed. No invented client figures.
7. How long it stays: single-shot selfie or desk talking head = the title stays the whole video. Produced or multi-scene video = the title stays on the opening shot and leaves at the first cut away (screen share, B-roll, second scene).
8. Never over the face. Top of the box at or below 285 px of 1920 (R-101). Captions are separate and smaller (see section 3).

## 2. Ten wording templates (rewritten for our niche)

| ID | Template | His pattern (paraphrased) | Our example |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Money proof | [Who] [did / does] [real $ result] 🤯 | role + monthly income + 🤯 | "Trading Discord owner hits [sourced $]/month 🤯" (organic only, needs a proof-library source) |
| T2 Dead | [Common thing] is DEAD ☠️❌ | platform is dead | "Free Discord links are DEAD ☠️❌" |
| T3 Worst | The WORST [thing] [of all time / you can make] ❌ | worst sale of all time | "The WORST way to grow your Discord ❌" |
| T4 Numbered | [N] [Things] to [Result] 🔥 + list builds on screen as spoken | N hooks / ways / formats | "5 Numbers to Know Before You Scale Your Discord 🔥" |
| T5 Blueprint | How to build a [result] [thing] + "(Full Blueprint)" | how to build a 1M business | "How we did $20M+ for trading Discords (Full Blueprint)" |
| T6 Broke vs Rich | Broke [role] ❌ / Rich [role] ✅ (split labels, numbers or behaviour under each) | broke vs rich coach / salesman | "Broke trading educator ❌ / Rich trading educator ✅" |
| T7 POV / be like | POV: [avatar moment] or [Avatar] be like... | POV business owners; entrepreneurs be like | "POV: I run your Discord's funnel for 30 days" |
| T8 Confession | I/we [real $ result] and this is the most [feeling] part of [topic]... | made X last month, most messed-up part | "We've done $20M+ for trading Discords and this is why most launches flop..." |
| T9 Objection | Overcoming [X] Objection 🔥 + line 2 the exact objection in quotes | sales objection + "Can I get a discount?" | "Overcoming the Price Objection 🔥 / "I can't afford your mentorship"" |
| T10 Quote setup | "[What people say to you]" + line 2 "My [reality]:" | "you're so good at AI" / My prompts: | ""Your Discord must run itself"" / "My funnel:" |

Pick the template from the script's first line: a list -> T4; a how-to/system -> T5; a contrarian take -> T2 or T3; a comparison -> T6; a story or confession -> T8; an objection -> T9; a skit -> T7 or T10. Write 3 titles per script (three templates), and the A/B arms decide.

## 3. Four looks (font, place, colour)

| Look | What it looks like | Where | Use with |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 Native white box | Instagram's own text tool look: black bold sans on a white rounded box, each line its own box, centred | upper-middle: chest height, or just above the head | T1, T5, T9, T10 |
| V2 Plain bold white | bold white sans, soft drop shadow, no box, centred | top third, above the head | T2, T3, T7, T8 |
| V3 Outline listicle | bold rounded white with a thick black outline, centred title; numbered list in 1-2 left columns that fill in as each item is spoken | title top third; list left side | T4 |
| V4 Condensed caps | tall condensed ALL CAPS white, one keyword yellow (#FFD400); sub-line bold white with yellow | top | cinematic / B-roll openers; T6 labels use V3 style with ❌ / ✅ |

Captions (separate from the title): bold white, soft shadow, 2-4 words, sentence case, lower-middle (chest), never on the face.

In our render tools: Inter Bold/ExtraBold for V1-V3, Inter Display Black (scaled tall) for V4, Noto Color Emoji. In Instagram's app: the native text tool ("Classic" with the background toggle for V1, "Strong"/"Modern" for V2/V3).

## 4. Paid ads

Paid-ad copy rules still win on ads (Video edit playbook 14.x and the claude-editor ad rules): the "For Trading Discord Owners" banner stays, no make/earn/money, no per-month figure in the first 5 s, no $ figure beside a timeframe in the first 15 s. So on ads use T2, T3, T4, T6 (no $), T7, T9, T10, and skip T1 and T8. The hook title replaces the old per-ad popup line ("Why most don't grow").

## 6. What the evidence does and doesn't say

Our 28 Oct hook study (3,348 reels) found no text formula beats chance on new reels; the one cue that helped is a real money figure or result in line one. T1, T5 and T8 carry one. These templates are Andy's style call; the creative scorecard and reel A/B arms judge them by booked calls, not views.

## 7. In build_cut.py

Config keys: `hook` (title text, `\N` = line break), `hook_look` V1-V4 (default V1), `hook_sub` (bracket sub-line), `*word*` turns a word yellow (V4), `hook_end` "full" (default, whole video) or a second (first cut away). `legacy` re-renders old drafts only. Tested 10 Nov on a synthetic 4.5 s clip: all four looks render and the title holds through the end hold. libass does not draw colour emoji: add the emoji in Instagram's text tool or as a PNG overlay, and check it on the render.
