About BusinessGrower.io

BusinessGrower.io is white-labeled GoHighLevel software, already branded for your business. It's the same software we build our Done-For-You funnels in. It's for trading Discord owners and trading educators who want one account for their lead magnets, email, SMS, booked calls and pipeline, instead of stitching together dozens of tools.

Who it's for

If you run a trading Discord or sell trading education, your back end runs off platform: somewhere to collect leads, follow up with them and book calls before the sale. This software lets you:

  • Build lead magnet pages and sales funnels, so people get a checklist, a PDF or a free Discord invite in exchange for an email
  • Send email sequences that move new leads through your YouTube videos in order, plus text follow-ups
  • Book high-ticket calls straight from the funnel
  • Track every lead in a visual pipeline: who opted in, who booked and who paid
  • Keep it all in one account and one dashboard, instead of a page tool, an email app, a texting app and a spreadsheet that don't talk to each other

Every trading Discord funnel comes down to traffic, conversion and LTV. This software handles the capture and follow-up: it collects the lead off platform, follows up, books the call and keeps a record of who bought. It doesn't drive traffic for you.

Plug and play

It comes white-labeled, already branded for your business and fully customizable, set up around how trading Discords and educators run: a lead magnet, email and Discord follow-up, then a booked call or a Whop offer.

Whether you run a Discord server, host live sessions, sell a course or offer mentorship, the aim is a back end that just works, so you spend your time educating and getting more people in. Anybody can get a funnel built on Fiverr or Upwork. The hard part is knowing what actually gets trading communities to perform.

The software is $197 a month or $1,200 a year. Texts, emails sent and calls are billed by usage.
